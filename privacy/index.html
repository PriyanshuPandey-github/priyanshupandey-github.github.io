Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 Developer: PotatoPanic Contact: ackgame01@gmail.com Android package: com.flight.fever

Flight Traffic Fever is a local puzzle game. You can play without creating an account with us. Advertising features require an internet connection.

Game information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, settings, power inventory, reward claims, spin cooldowns and your selected age range on your device. It uses age ranges rather than asking for your full date of birth. This information is used to save your progress, apply game rules and configure advertising treatment. We do not operate a gameplay server or upload your saved game to our own server in the current release. Clearing the app's storage removes its local saved information and resets progress.

Advertising and consent

The advertising-enabled version uses Google AdMob to display Home banners, occasional interstitials between completed levels and optional rewarded ads. A reward is applied only after the advertising SDK confirms completion. Ad availability depends on connectivity, consent and other eligibility conditions.

Advertising services can process network information such as IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The data processed depends on your region, privacy choices, age treatment and device settings. These services have their own data handling and retention practices. Read Google's Privacy Policy and information about Google's advertising services.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent interface and provides Privacy choices on Home. You can change your device-local age choice in Ad age settings. Selecting Continue without ads prevents advertising requests until you change that choice. The app configures child-directed and under-age treatment for the corresponding age groups and restricts requested ad content rating. The build distributed with all ads disabled excludes the advertising SDKs and does not request ads.

Purchases and accounts

Google Play purchases are disabled in the current configuration. The app does not currently require Play Games login or collect payment-card details. Before enabling purchases, the developer will update this policy to explain Google Play processing, purchase restoration and any purchase-verification data actually used.

Permissions and other services

The app uses internet access for advertising and related service communication. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location. The current app does not include a separate developer analytics service or a developer account system.

Your choices and contact

You can manage advertising identifiers through Android settings, change available in-app privacy choices, clear the app's local storage or uninstall the app. Google-held data is subject to Google's controls and policies. If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your request. Contact ackgame01@gmail.com with privacy questions, including concerns about children's information.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The current policy and effective date will be available at this page.
